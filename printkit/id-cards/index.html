Print Kit

ID card maker

Paste a list of names and print cards at the usual card size, with a box for a photo.

Before you print

  • Put only what is needed: no identity numbers and no home addresses.
  • Check every name and number. A misprinted card is a wasted card.
  • Print one test page on plain paper and check the cutting size.

Read ID card basics for size, privacy, photos and control of cards. Starting lists for a school, a coaching centre, an office and a club are under examples.

Common questions

Is the list sent to a server?

No. The cards are made in your browser and the list is not stored or sent anywhere.

Can I add photos?

The card has a photo box. Print a passport photo and stick it, or fill it in your own editor.
