Course and class page maker

Describe your class once and share one link that shows the details and lets students pay and join.

Before you share it

  • Check the fee and the dates, and open the link on your own phone.
  • Check that the Pay button shows your name and the right amount in your UPI app. Not every phone opens UPI links, so also give your UPI id in the chat.
  • Check every payment in your own bank app before you confirm a place.

Read writing a page that sells a class and selling classes with UPI safely.

Common questions

Do you handle the payment?

No. The Pay button opens the customer's own UPI app and the money goes from them to you. We never touch it and cannot see it.

What if I do not want to show a Pay button?

Leave the UPI id empty. The page then shows a button to message you on WhatsApp.
