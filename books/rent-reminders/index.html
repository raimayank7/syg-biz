Rent reminder messages that keep the peace

A good tenant is worth keeping. A calm, specific message gets paid without a quarrel.

First reminder (a few days late)

Hello [name], a gentle reminder that the rent of [amount] for [month] is due. Please pay by [UPI or bank details] when you can. Thank you.

Second reminder (a week late)

Hello [name], the rent for [month] is still pending: [amount]. Please let me know when I can expect it. If there is any difficulty, I am happy to talk.

Part payment received

Thank you [name], I have received [amount] for [month]. The balance is [balance]. Please let me know when you will pay it.

Thank you

Thank you [name], received [amount] for [month]. Have a good day.

How to send them

  • Use one chat per tenant, and never remind in a group.
  • Always state the month and the amount.
  • Send at a sensible hour, not late at night.
  • Keep a copy: the rent book opens WhatsApp with the message already written, and shows who is late.

Know your limits

A reminder is not a legal notice. If rent stays unpaid for long, ask a lawyer about the right steps where you live, and do not take actions such as cutting the water or power or locking the tenant out, which may be illegal.

Common questions

Should I charge a late fee?

Only if the agreement says so. Local rules differ, so check before you add one.

What if the tenant does not reply?

Call, then write a short message with a date. Keep the messages in case you need to show them later.
