Asking for reviews honestly

Reviews bring new customers, and people can tell when they are real. Honest asking gets honest reviews.

When to ask

Right after a good moment: a finished job, a meal enjoyed, a delivery arrived. A short message or a QR card at the counter works best, because the memory is fresh.

How to ask

Use the review link from your own business profile. The review request kit makes a QR card and messages in English, Hindi and Punjabi from that link. Ask everyone, in the same words.

What not to do

  • Do not ask only happy customers. Selecting who to ask is called review gating and platform rules generally forbid it.
  • Do not pay or reward for reviews, or ask people to give five stars.
  • Do not write reviews for your own place or ask friends and staff to do so.
  • Do not threaten or argue with someone who left a bad review.

Platform rules change and differ, so read the current policy of the platform where you ask for reviews.

Replying

Thank you [name] for your kind words. We are glad you enjoyed [item or service]. We hope to see you again.

Thank you for telling us, [name], and we are sorry about your experience. We would like to put it right. Please call us on [number] so that we can talk.

Reply to good reviews briefly and to bad ones calmly, once. Other customers read the reply as much as the review.

Learn from the reviews

Write the common complaints in a list, and fix the one that appears most.

Common questions

How many reviews do I need?

There is no fixed number. A steady flow of recent, honest reviews matters more than a high count. Ask every customer and let the number grow.

What if I get a fake review?

Report it on the platform, and reply politely. Do not retaliate.
