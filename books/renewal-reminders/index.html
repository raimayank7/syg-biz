Renewal reminders that work

Most members who leave never decided to; they simply forgot. A well-timed message brings many back.

When to send

  • Five days before the end: a friendly reminder with the end date.
  • On the last day: a short note and how to renew.
  • One week after: a "we miss you" message.
  • One month after: a last, warm invitation, maybe with a small offer.

Messages

Hello [name], your membership ends on [date]. It has been good to see you! To renew, just reply or drop by. Thank you.

Hello [name], we have not seen you for a while. If life got busy, no problem. Your first class back is on us if you come this week.

Do not guilt-trip

Members respond to warmth, not pressure. Avoid messages that blame or compare. Mention what they have achieved if you know it.

Use the numbers

The tracker shows memberships ending within a week and those that have lapsed. If many lapse after the first month, the first month experience needs attention: a welcome plan, a check-in at week two, or a buddy.

Common questions

Should I give a discount to bring back lapsed members?

A small one can help, but giving it to everyone teaches people to wait. Use it for people who left a while ago.

Is it all right to message members on WhatsApp?

Yes for matters about their membership, when they gave you their number for that purpose. Stop if they ask.
