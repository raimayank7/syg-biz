How it is worked out

The instalment is the loan times r times (1 + r) to the power n, divided by ((1 + r) to the power n minus 1), where r is the rate for one month (the yearly rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of months. A loan of 300,000 at 12 percent for 3 years has an instalment of about 9,964.

Before you borrow

  • Check that the business profit, after your own pay, is clearly above the instalment.
  • Ask for all fees and the total you will repay.
  • Borrow the least you need.

Details of government loan schemes change, so this site does not describe them: ask a bank or the official scheme page.

Common questions

Is this the same as the bank figure?

It should be close if the bank uses a monthly reducing balance. Fees, rounding and other methods can change it slightly.

Can I borrow without collateral?

It depends on the lender and the scheme. Ask the bank; this site does not state rules.
