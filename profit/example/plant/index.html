Profit on plants and garden items: a worked example

How this kind of product earns or loses money online, with example figures you can replace by your own.

How this product sells

Live plants are a perishable product: they must reach the customer fresh, survive a trip in a box and are often replaced for free if they die. Packaging and replacement are costs most new sellers forget.

What to watch

Live-arrival rules of the platform and the courier, the share of orders that need a replacement, weather delays, packing that protects roots and leaves, and which plants may be shipped across state or country borders.

A tip for this product

Offer a replacement for plants that arrive damaged, and put its expected cost into the price. Ask the customer for a photo on arrival so you can learn which species travel badly.

Common questions

Why is the pack cost so high?

Plants need rigid boxes, moisture and padding. A box that is cheap for a book is not enough for a plant.

Can I ship plants anywhere?

No. Some places restrict moving plants and soil. Check the rules of the places you ship to before you list.
