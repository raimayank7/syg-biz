Bundle pricing calculator

Customers like a saving and you like a bigger order. This checks that the bundle still earns what you need.

What the sum leaves out

This sum is on product profit only. Add the platform fees, shipping and GST arithmetic from the profit calculator for each case to see the final figure. Fees as a percentage fall on the bundle price, so a bundle with a 10 percent discount also pays 10 percent less commission on the same goods.

Which products to bundle

  • Items that are used together: a case and a screen guard, a candle and a holder.
  • A fast seller with a slow one, so the slow one moves.
  • Items of similar weight, so shipping does not jump a band.

Names and listings

Give the bundle a clear name that says what is in it and the saving. Never mix products so that the customer cannot tell what they receive.

Common questions

Why do bundles raise the profit per order?

Fixed fees, shipping and the cost of winning the order are paid once. Selling two items in one parcel usually costs less than twice as much to handle.

How big should a bundle discount be?

Small enough that the bundle still earns more than selling either item alone. The calculator shows both profits so you can see the trade.
