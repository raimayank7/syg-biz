Common questions

Is it free?

Yes, with no account.

Where do answers go?

To your WhatsApp. Nothing is stored by us.

Do you handle payments?

No. The course page can show a Pay by UPI button that opens the customer's own UPI app. The money goes from them to you.

Everything a visitor types is packed into the link and nothing is stored by us; answers and replies go to the sender's WhatsApp. The UPI link opens a UPI app with the amount filled in and has not been tested with every app. Example names, prices and dates are made up.
