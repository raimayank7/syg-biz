Lead stages and your win rate

Five stages are enough for most small businesses. What matters is that each one means the same thing every time.

The five stages

StageWhat it meansWhat moves it on
NewSomeone asked, you have not replied yetYou reply
ContactedYou have talked or messaged and know what they needYou send a price or a plan
Quote sentThey have a price in writingThey say yes or no
WonThey agreed or paidThe job is done
LostThey said no, or stopped replying after your last follow-upWrite the reason in the note

Reading your win rate

The tracker shows how many closed leads you won. If you closed 20 leads and won 6, your win rate is 30 percent. That number tells you what a lead is worth: if an average sale is 5,000 and you win 30 percent, each lead is worth about 1,500 before costs. It tells you how much you can afford to spend to get a lead.

Using the numbers

  • If many leads are stuck at "Quote sent", your price or your follow-up needs attention.
  • If many are lost with the same note, such as "too far", that is a signal about your offer or your area.
  • If few leads arrive, look at "where they came from" to see which source works.

Keep it honest

Move a lead to Lost when you are sure, so the list stays clean and the win rate is true. A list full of dead leads hides the live ones. See the follow-up routine for what to do before you give up on a lead.

Common questions

Can I add my own stages?

This tracker uses five fixed stages to keep the page simple. Use the note for more detail.

Where do I record why I lost a lead?

In the note. Write one short reason and read them once a month.
