Day rate: how to set it

A day rate is usually your hourly floor times the hours you will bill in a day, not times 24 or times a full 8 if some of the day goes on calls and messages. Say what a day is in your quote. A half day is half the day rate plus a small premium, because short bookings are harder to fill.

Monthly income is not hourly rate times 160

An employee earns for about 160 hours a month. A freelancer bills far fewer. Use the billable hours guide to find your share and the rate calculator to work backwards from the income you want.

Common questions

Why is my yearly income lower than the table says?

The table assumes every hour is billed. Multiply by your billable share (often 40 to 60 percent) for a realistic figure.

How many hours is a "day"?

Decide a day and tell clients, for example 8 hours including breaks. Half days then count as 4 hours.
