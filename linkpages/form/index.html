Link Pages

Form and survey maker

Write your questions, get a link, and receive the answers on WhatsApp.

How the question lines work

  • How was the food? | rating gives buttons 1 to 5.
  • Would you come again? | choice: Yes, Maybe, No gives options to tap.
  • Your name | text is a one-line answer, and Comments | long is a bigger box.

Use up to 20 questions, but fewer is better: see feedback questions that get answers. Examples for cafes, clinics, complaints, enquiries, RSVPs, orders and staff surveys are under form examples.

Answers are sent as a WhatsApp message to your number, so they arrive one by one in your chat. If you need answers in a sheet, copy them in yourself.

Common questions

Is anything stored on a server?

No. The form is packed into the link and the answers go straight to your WhatsApp.

Can I see the answers in a table?

Not here. Copy them from WhatsApp into a sheet. A server-based form tool is a better fit if you need a table automatically.
