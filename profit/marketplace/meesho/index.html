Working out profit when you sell on Meesho

The kinds of charges to look for, and how to turn them into a profit figure.

Independent site, not connected with Meesho. No Meesho fee or rate is stated here; read them on the official page: supplier.meesho.com. Fee names are general and may change.

How selling works here

Meesho connects suppliers with resellers who sell to customers through social media; suppliers and resellers earn in different ways.

The fees to expect

Look for whatever the supplier panel shows for your category: charges for shipping and returns, any commission, and the reseller margin you must leave in your price.

What to look up before you list

Write down: whether you sell as a supplier or as a reseller, the shipping charge by weight, the return rate in your category and the payout schedule.

Fill the calculator in this order

  1. Cost of the unit and packaging, from your own books.
  2. Commission and other percent fees, from the rate card.
  3. Fixed fee and shipping, from the charges for your weight and area.
  4. GST or sales tax on the product and on the fees, from your tax adviser.
  5. Return rate and loss per return, from your last few weeks of orders or from similar products.
  6. Advertising per order, from your ad report.

Then open the profit calculator and compare the profit with what the platform pays you in the first settlement. If they differ, find the line that explains the difference.

Common questions

Does this page list Meesho fees?

No. Fees and rates change and differ by category, so the page lists the kinds of fee to look up and where. Type the real numbers from your own seller account into the calculator.

Is this site connected with Meesho?

No. This is an independent site. The name is used only to say where the fees are, and belongs to its owner.
