Link Pages

Event invitation page maker

Fill in the details once. Guests get a page with a calendar file, a map link and an RSVP button.

What guests can do

  • Read the date, time, place and details in plain words.
  • Add the event to their phone calendar with one tap.
  • Open the place on a map.
  • Tell you on WhatsApp that they will come and how many are coming, or that they cannot.

Read invitation wording, RSVP and headcount and the event day checklist. This is not a ticketing tool: it does not sell tickets or check people in.

Common questions

Can it sell tickets?

No. It shows the details and collects replies on WhatsApp. For paid classes, use the course page, which has a UPI pay button.

Does the calendar file work on every phone?

Most phones open an .ics file and offer to add it to the calendar. Times are local, so the event appears at the same clock time wherever the phone is.
