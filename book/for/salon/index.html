Booking page for salon and parlour

What to put in the form, and what to watch for, if you run this kind of business.

Setting up the page

A salon sells many short services, so the main decision is the slot step. Use a step of 15 minutes so a 15-minute threading and a 90-minute colour both fit the same page, and add a gap of 10 minutes for cleaning the chair and tools.

What goes wrong, and how to handle it

Long services such as hair colour or bridal work block the whole afternoon. Put those on fewer days or ask for an advance, and keep short services for busy weekdays.

No-shows

No-shows hurt most when a long service goes empty. Send a reminder the evening before and ask the customer to reply to confirm; free the slot if there is no reply by morning.

One more thing

Add the stylist's name to the service ("Haircut with Ravi") if each person has their own queue, and make one link for each stylist.

Common questions

Can I show different prices for men and women?

Yes. Add them as separate services, for example "Haircut (men)" and "Haircut (women)", each with its own time and price.

How do I block my day off?

Untick that day of the week, or list a single date under days off in the maker.
