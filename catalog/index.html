Free catalogue maker for your shop

Add your items, prices and photos. Get a catalogue to print or save as a PDF, a price list for WhatsApp and an order link where customers choose and send their order.

Three ways to share

  • PDF or printout with photos: the best for items sold by looks. See the catalogue PDF tips.
  • WhatsApp price list as text: the quickest to send and to update.
  • Order link without photos: customers add quantities and send you a ready list with a total.

A good first catalogue

  1. Start with your 10 best sellers, not everything.
  2. Take photos in daylight with a plain background. See product photos.
  3. Write one clear line for each item. See writing descriptions.
  4. Show clear prices. See showing prices.
  5. Test the order link on your own phone before you share it.

Pick your kind of shop under by shop type for example items to start from.

Common questions

Is the catalogue maker free?

Yes, with no account.

Where are my photos kept?

In your own browser on this device. They are shrunk before they are saved. Download the catalogue file to keep a copy.

Why does the order link have no photos?

A link cannot carry photos. Use the PDF or the printout for pictures, and the link for choosing quantities and sending the order.

Does it take payments?

No. Customers send an order on WhatsApp, and you confirm the total and take payment in your usual way.
