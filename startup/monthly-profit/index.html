Monthly profit and break-even worksheet

Type the numbers you have found in your test. See the profit, the break-even sales and what a slow month does.

These numbers are examples. Use numbers from your own test of the idea, not hopes.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Sales a month = sales a day x days open.
  • Left after the cost of what you sell = sales x (price minus cost per sale).
  • Profit before your pay = that figure minus fixed costs and the loan instalment.
  • Break-even sales = (fixed costs + instalment) / (price minus cost per sale).

Test the plan with 70 percent of your sales figure to see the business in a slow month. If it loses money, find a way to cut fixed costs or raise the price before you start. For prices see the freelance rate calculator and the seller profit calculators.

Common questions

Where do I get "sales a day"?

From your small test and from watching similar places. Count customers for a week. Do not use a guess you want to be true.

Is my own pay a cost?

Yes. Count it, or you will think the business earns more than it does.
