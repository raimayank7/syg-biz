Fee receipt maker

A clean one-page receipt for a parent, in a few seconds.

Tips

Number your receipts in order and keep a copy. If you take fees by UPI, the bank record also proves the payment. The manager prints a receipt straight from the fees tab for the amount paid so far.

What a good receipt shows

The date, who paid and for whom, the month the money is for, the amount in numbers, how it was paid and your signature. If it is a part payment, write the balance. For a cheque, note the number and the bank. Parents rarely ask for a receipt, but the day they do, a clear one prevents an argument.

Keep your copy

Print two, or take a photo of the signed receipt before you hand it over. At the end of the year, the stack of receipts or photos is your proof of what you earned, which an accountant will need if you pay tax on your income.

Common questions

Is this receipt valid for tax purposes?

It is a simple acknowledgement of payment. Tax and invoice rules differ by country, so ask an accountant if you need a formal invoice.
