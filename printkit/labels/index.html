Print Kit

Price tag and barcode label maker

Type your items, choose the layout and print. Barcodes are drawn in your browser.

Test before you print many. The barcodes are drawn by this page's own code and have not been tested with a scanner by us. Print one page and scan a few labels with the scanner or phone you will use.

How to use it

  1. Type one item on each line: name, comma, price, comma, code.
  2. Choose price tag, barcode only or shelf label.
  3. Choose the number of labels across to match your sticker sheet.
  4. Press Print, and in the print window choose A4 and scale 100 percent.

New to barcodes? Read barcode basics, label sheet sizes and price tag design. Single barcodes can be made on the barcode maker.

Common questions

Is my list uploaded anywhere?

No. Labels are made in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

What if a code cannot be made into a barcode?

The label shows a red note. EAN-13 needs 12 or 13 digits with a right last digit; Code 128 needs letters, digits or common symbols without accents.
