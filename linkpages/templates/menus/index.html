Menu page examples

Three kinds of food business and how each lays out the menu.

The names, prices and dates are made up. The button opens the builder with the example filled in, and you replace the words with yours.

Menu page for a small restaurant

A restaurant menu on a phone replaces the paper one and lets guests send the order from the table. Sections keep a long list readable, and the veg marker is the first thing most Indian diners look for.

Tip. Put the QR on each table and at the door. Ask guests to give the table number, and keep a printed menu for those who do not use phones.

Weekly menu for a home cook or tiffin seller

A home cook changes the menu every week, so a link page that you can remake in a minute beats printing. The section names show which day each dish is cooked.

Tip. Say the order cut-off time in the first line and keep the number of meals you can cook each day in mind: stop taking orders when you reach it.

Menu page for a bakery or cake maker

A bakery sells ready items and made-to-order cakes. A page with prices for the standard cakes and a line about notice for special ones answers most calls.

Tip. For custom cakes, ask people to message the design and weight before ordering, and take an advance. State any allergy information clearly.
