Profit on t-shirts and clothing: a worked example

How this kind of product earns or loses money online, with example figures you can replace by your own.

How this product sells

Clothing is a size-and-fit product. Customers cannot try it on, so many order two sizes and send one back, or return it for a colour that looks different on screen. Returns are the biggest hidden cost.

What to watch

Return rate by size, packaging that keeps a folded shirt clean, a size chart in the photos, fabric weight, colour accuracy in photos, seasonal stock that does not sell after the season.

A tip for this product

Put a measurement chart in the second photo, in centimetres across the chest and length. It is the cheapest way to lower returns, and every return you avoid saves the shipping both ways.

Common questions

Why is the return rate so high in the example?

Clothing is often ordered in more than one size. The example uses 18 percent for illustration; your own return report in the seller account gives the real figure, and it can be higher or lower by category.

Should I add a size chart image?

Yes. A clear chart and a model photo with the model height and size cut wrong-size orders, which are the most common reason for a return.
