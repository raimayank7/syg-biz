Print Kit

Printing labels, cards and posters well

Most print problems are settings, not printers. A test page saves a sheet of stickers.

Settings that matter

  • Paper size: A4 unless you buy something else.
  • Scale: 100 percent, never "fit to page".
  • Margins: default or none, and be consistent.
  • Quality: normal is enough for text; use high for barcodes and photos.
  • Background graphics: turn on if your colours do not print.

Always print a test

Print on plain paper, hold it over the real sheet against a window, and check that the borders line up. Then print on the sticker sheet or card.

Barcodes

Print them black on white, not in colour or grey, with a blank space on both sides. Do not shrink them: a small barcode that scans badly costs more than the paper you save. Test-scan after printing.

Home printer or print shop?

NeedBetter choice
Ten cards or one sheet of labelsHome printer
Hundreds of cards, glossy finish, cut to sizePrint shop
Large posterPrint shop (the A4 file scales up)
Cards that must lastPrint on thick card and laminate

Cutting and finishing

Use a steel ruler and a sharp cutter over a mat, not scissors. Laminate cards with pouches for strength, and round the corners for a neat finish.

Common questions

Why are my colours different from the screen?

Screens and printers use different colour methods. A test print shows the real colour, and a print shop can match it better.

Can I print from my phone?

Yes, if the printer supports it. Otherwise save the file and print from a computer.
