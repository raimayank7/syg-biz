Messages for parents: fee, absence, test and more

Polite, short messages a tutor can copy and send, in English, Hindi and Punjabi. Fill the brackets and send.

Hindi and Punjabi texts were written by us and not read by a native speaker. Read them before you send.

Fee reminder

English Hello [parent], this is a gentle reminder that the tuition fee of [amount] for [student] for [month] is due. You can pay by UPI to [upi] or in cash at the class. Thank you. - [teacher]

Hindi नमस्ते [parent] जी, [student] की [month] की ट्यूशन फ़ीस [amount] बाकी है। कृपया [upi] पर UPI से या कक्षा में नकद दे दें। धन्यवाद। - [teacher]

Tips for sending

  • Send fee reminders in the morning or early evening, not late at night.
  • Name the month and the amount, and say how to pay.
  • Send private concerns to the parent alone, never in a group.

Common questions

How do I fill the brackets?

Replace each word in square brackets with the real name, amount or date before you send. The tuition manager fills them for you when you use the Remind button.

Are the Hindi and Punjabi messages checked?

They were written by us and have not been read by a native speaker. Please read each one and change any word you would not use.
