Taking class fees by UPI safely

A UPI link makes paying easy. Confirming that the money arrived is still your job.

What the Pay button does

The course page has a Pay by UPI button. On a phone it opens the customer's own UPI app with your UPI id, the name and the amount already filled in. The customer checks the details and pays in the app, with their own PIN. We never see or touch the money; it goes straight from the customer to you.

The link uses the common UPI payment address format. We have not tested it with every UPI app, and some apps or phones may not open it. Always offer a second way to pay, such as showing your UPI id in the WhatsApp reply.

Confirm every payment

  1. The customer sends you a message on WhatsApp after paying.
  2. Open your own bank or UPI app and check that the money has arrived. Do not trust a screenshot alone.
  3. Reply with the confirmation and the joining details.
  4. Write the name and amount in your list.

Receipts and records

Give a receipt if asked. The free receipt maker on Nivesh Sahayak makes one in a minute. Keep a record of every payment for your own accounts; rules on tax and invoices differ by country and this site does not state them.

Refunds

Write your refund rule in the description: for example, a full refund before the class starts and none after. Refund quickly and by the same route.

Scams to avoid

  • Never ask a customer to pay to someone else's account.
  • Never enter your PIN or OTP to receive money. Receiving never needs a PIN.
  • Be careful with "send me a collect request" messages from strangers.
  • Do not click payment links that you did not make.

Common questions

Does the link work on a computer?

The UPI button works on phones that have a UPI app. On a computer the customer should use the UPI id you give in the message.

Do I pay any fee to use the link page?

No fee is charged by this site. Your bank or UPI app may have its own rules; ask them.
