Common questions

Is Print Kit free?

Yes, with no account.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything is made in your browser. The only things that leave are files you download or print.

Are the barcodes tested?

They are drawn by our own code and checked for structure, but we had no scanner to test them. Print a sheet and scan a few before you print many.

Everything on this site is made in your browser: nothing you type or upload is sent to us. Barcodes are made by this page's own code: print one sheet and test-scan a few before you print many.
