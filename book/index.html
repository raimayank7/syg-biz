How customers use it

  1. They open your link and choose a service.
  2. They choose one of the days you are open and a free time.
  3. They type their name and number and press the button.
  4. WhatsApp opens with a ready message to you. They send it, and you confirm by reply.

It is simple on purpose. Read how it works and what it cannot do before you give out the link, and use the no-show policy wording to protect your time. Starting points for ten kinds of business are under by business.

Common questions

Is the booking page free?

Yes. There is no account and no payment. You get a link to share.

Does it block times that are already booked?

Not by itself. It cannot know who booked. After you confirm a booking, add the time to the "already taken" box and share the new link, or just tell the customer if two ask for the same time.

Where do customers' details go?

Into a WhatsApp message to your number. We never see them.

Can customers book in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Choose the language of the customer page in the maker.
