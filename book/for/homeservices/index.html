Booking page for plumbers, electricians and home repairs

What to put in the form, and what to watch for, if you run this kind of business.

Setting up the page

Home service jobs depend on travel and on the job being as expected. Use longer slots with a gap equal to your usual travel time, and ask for the area in the note so you do not take bookings you cannot reach.

What goes wrong, and how to handle it

A "one hour" job can turn into a half day. Tell customers that the time is the start of the visit and that the work may take longer, and keep a buffer in the evening.

No-shows

Customers who are not at home waste your trip. Ask them to confirm the address and a phone number that will be answered, and charge a small visiting fee that is adjusted against the job.

One more thing

List the areas you cover and the usual visiting charge in the business note.

Common questions

Should I show prices?

Show the visiting or estimate charge. Final prices depend on the work, so say that clearly.

How do I handle emergencies like a burst pipe?

Put your phone number in the business note and say that urgent jobs should be called in, not booked.
