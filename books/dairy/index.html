Milk collection book

Daily milk entries by farmer with fat and SNF, a rate worked out from your own price per fat point, and a monthly bill.

What it is for

Milk is bought by litres and fat, and the farmer's bill at month end is the sum of many small entries. The book works out each entry from your own rates and totals each farmer's month.. It is built for a small dairy, a milk collection centre or a milkman who buys from farmers.

How to use it

  1. Open "Your rates" and write the price for each 1% of fat (and SNF if you use it).
  2. For every collection add the farmer, date, litres, fat and, if you test it, SNF.
  3. The rate and the amount are worked out for each entry.
  4. At month end choose the month and read each farmer's total litres, average fat and amount.

Common questions

Where are the records kept?

In your own browser on this device. Download a backup file now and then.

Can I move the data to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Use "Download as a spreadsheet file (.csv)" at the bottom of the register.
