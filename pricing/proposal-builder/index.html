Freelance proposal builder

Type the job once. Get a clear one-page proposal your client can read and sign.

What a good proposal says

  1. The client's problem and what you will achieve.
  2. What is included and what is not.
  3. How long it takes and when you start.
  4. The price in lines the client can follow, and how it is paid.
  5. How many revisions are included.
  6. How long the offer is valid.

Work out the numbers first with the project price calculator, and use the wording in quote wording. For a tax invoice or a formal quotation with your logo, the free makers on Nivesh Sahayak help. This is not a legal contract: for large jobs, have a lawyer review your terms.

Common questions

Is anything stored?

No. The proposal is made in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

Is it a contract?

It is a clear written offer that a client can accept by signing or replying. For a large or risky job, use a contract reviewed by a lawyer.
