Catalogue, WhatsApp Business or a website?

Start with the simplest tool that sells. Move to a bigger one only when the simple one starts to cost you time or orders.

How to decide

  • Fewer than 30 items and mostly regular customers: a catalogue and WhatsApp are enough.
  • Customers ask for stock and payment online: think about a shop.
  • Orders are more than you can answer by hand: move to a system that records orders for you.

Do both

Many small shops use a WhatsApp catalogue for regulars and a simple website or a marketplace page for new customers. The same photos and prices can serve both, so keep them in one file: the catalogue file you can download from the maker.

Cost and fees

This site does not list fees of any platform. If you plan to sell on a marketplace or your own shop, use the seller profit calculator to check what you keep after fees.

An independent site

This site is independent of WhatsApp and of any shop builder. Names are used only to describe what each option does, and belong to their owners.

Common questions

Do I need a website?

Not at first. Many shops work well with WhatsApp and a catalogue for years. A website helps when new customers need to find you by search.

Is the catalogue link like a website?

It is a simple page that shows your list and sends orders to your WhatsApp. It has no payments, no stock and no search by Google.
