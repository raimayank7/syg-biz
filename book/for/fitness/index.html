Booking page for yoga, fitness and personal trainers

What to put in the form, and what to watch for, if you run this kind of business.

Setting up the page

Most trainers work in an early morning and an evening block. Set the open hours wide and add the quiet midday hours to the days-off list, or make two links, one for morning and one for evening.

What goes wrong, and how to handle it

A group class has a fixed time and many people, while a personal session has one person. This tool is built for one booking per slot, so use it for personal sessions and trials, and a message group for group classes.

No-shows

Ask people to cancel at least a few hours before and say what you do if they do not: for example, the session is counted as taken.

One more thing

For a package of ten sessions, agree the plan and fees by chat once, then use the page only to choose each session's time.

Read how the booking link works for the limits, and booking messages for words to send.

Common questions

Can I stop people booking a time that I am teaching a group?

Add that time to the "already taken" box in the maker for each day, or choose open hours that leave it out.

What about people who ask for medical advice?

Say in the business note that you give fitness guidance only and that medical questions should go to a doctor.
