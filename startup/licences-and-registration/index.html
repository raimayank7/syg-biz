Finding out which licences you need

Rules depend on the kind of business, the place and the size. This page does not state any. It shows how to find out.

Why not guess

Working without a required licence can lead to fines or closure, and copying what a neighbour did may not apply to you. Rules also change.

Who to ask

  • The local municipal or panchayat office, for shop, trade and food activities.
  • The food safety authority, for anything you cook, pack or sell to eat.
  • The labour or industries office, for staff and workshops.
  • The tax authorities and a chartered accountant, for tax registration and returns.
  • The transport authority, for vehicles used for business.
  • The education authority, for coaching classes and schools.

What to ask

I want to start [describe the business] at [place], working from [home / shop], with [number] helpers. Which licences, registrations and permissions do I need, what do they cost and how long do they take?

Write it down

Keep a list of each requirement, who gave you the answer, the date and the cost. Ask for answers in writing where you can. Keep copies of every certificate.

Do it before the investment

Ask before you buy equipment or sign a lease. Some businesses cannot get permission at a given address, which is cheaper to learn first.

Common questions

Can I start from home?

Sometimes, depending on the business and local rules. Ask the authority before you begin.

Do I need an accountant?

Many small owners use one to register, file returns and keep accounts. Ask what they charge before you decide.
