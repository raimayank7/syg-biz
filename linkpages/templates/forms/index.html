Form examples

Seven forms you can open, change and share.

The names, prices and dates are made up. The button opens the builder with the example filled in, and you replace the words with yours.

Feedback form for a restaurant or cafe

Restaurant feedback should take under two minutes, or nobody finishes it. Three ratings, one yes or no and one open box give you numbers and a reason.

Tip. Put the form link on the bill or on a small card at the table, and read the open answers once a week. Fix one thing a week and tell regulars what changed.

Feedback form for a clinic (no health details)

A clinic should ask about service, never about health. The form says so at the top, so that patients do not send private details through a chat link.

Tip. Read the answers as a team and reply by phone to anyone who is unhappy. Never ask for symptoms, reports or photos in a form like this.

Complaint form

A complaint form makes it easy for an unhappy customer to tell you instead of telling everyone else. The answer to "what would you like us to do" gives you a fix to offer.

Tip. Reply the same day, even if only to say you have seen it. Write in your records what you did, and look for the same complaint twice.

Enquiry form

An enquiry form saves the back-and-forth of asking the same four questions in every chat: what, when, where and who.

Tip. Put the link in your profile and on posters. When the answer arrives, copy it into your lead list and follow up the next day.

RSVP form for a function or class

A headcount is needed days before an event. A form gets the answers in the same shape from everyone, which a group chat does not.

Tip. Send the link in the invitation and again three days before. Count the Yes answers and add a margin for the Maybe ones.

Simple order form

For a small seller with a short menu, an order form is quicker than a catalogue: it lists the five things you need to know to deliver.

Tip. Always confirm the order back with the total before you start. For a price list with quantities, use the catalogue maker of this family.

Open this example in the builder

Staff or member survey

A survey for staff or members is only useful if people feel safe answering. A line saying that the name is optional raises honest answers.

Tip. Read all the answers, thank people, and tell them one thing you will change. A survey with no follow-up teaches people not to answer next time.
