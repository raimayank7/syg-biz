Menu page and QR menu maker

Type your menu once. Get a link and a QR code; guests read it on their phone and send the order on WhatsApp.

How a guest uses it

  1. Scans the QR code or opens your link.
  2. Searches or scrolls, and taps + on the dishes.
  3. Gives a table number, or a name and address, and presses Send order on WhatsApp.

You receive the list and the total in your chat. Read writing a menu for a phone, pricing dishes and QR code basics. Examples are under menu examples. It is not a billing system: it does not take payment or tell the kitchen automatically.

Common questions

Is the menu stored on a server?

No. It is packed into the link and the QR code. Change the menu, and you must make a new link and print a new code.

Can I show dish photos?

No, photos cannot be carried in a link. Use the catalogue maker for a photo catalogue.
