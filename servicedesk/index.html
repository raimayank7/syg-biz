Registers for a service business

Five simple registers that show what needs attention today: contracts to renew, warranties and services due, rented items to collect, guests arriving and client jobs due. Your data stays on your device.

Pick a register

  • Service contract trackerAnnual maintenance contracts: who, what is covered, when each renews, visits due and money pending.
  • Warranty and service remindersProducts you sold, the day each warranty ends and the next service, with a message to the customer.
  • Rental registerItems out, who has them, the day they are due back, rent so far and deposits held.
  • Client work trackerJobs for clients with due dates, stage, fee and a message to ask for documents or remind: for small accountancy, tax and similar practices.
  • Guest registerGuests, rooms, check-in and check-out dates, who arrives and leaves today and what is still to collect.

How they work

Each register is a list with a form to add a row, a search box, a "Needs attention" panel for rows whose date has come and message buttons for WhatsApp. Records are saved in your browser, and you can download a backup or a spreadsheet file any time.

Common questions

Are the registers free?

Yes, with no account.

Where is my data kept?

In your own browser on your device. Download a backup file regularly.

Do they send messages by themselves?

No. A message button opens WhatsApp with the text ready, and you press send.
