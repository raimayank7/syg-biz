Print Kit

Visiting card maker

Type your details once. Get a printable card, a contact file and a link that works on any phone.

What you get

  • A printable sheet of ten cards at 86 by 54 mm.
  • A contact file (.vcf) that saves your details into any phone.
  • A link to a small card page with buttons for call, WhatsApp, email, website, map and save contact.

Your details are packed into the link and not stored on our side. Read what to put on a visiting card.

Common questions

Does the digital card need an account?

No. The link holds your details and opens a page on any phone.

How do I update my card?

Make a new link and remove the old link from your profile; old links keep showing the old details.
